# Personal Digital Marketing Portfolio — Content Blueprint

## Positioning
A personal professional portfolio, not an agency website. The aim is to present skills, practical work, experience, and an easy contact path.

**Suggested positioning line:** I help businesses strengthen their digital presence through thoughtful strategy, creative content, and data-informed marketing.

## About me
I'm Harsh Anand, a digital marketing professional interested in the intersection of creative communication and measurable business goals. I enjoy understanding an audience, shaping a clear message, and turning ideas into content and campaigns that feel relevant.

My focus includes Meta advertising, social media management, content planning, Google Ads, and website experiences. I approach every project with curiosity, clear communication, and a willingness to learn, test, and improve.

- **Meta Ads:** Campaign planning, audience research, creative testing, setup, optimization, and performance tracking.
- **Social Media Management:** Content calendars, account planning, publishing workflows, community engagement, and consistency.

## Project case study format
For each of 2–4 strong projects, include:
1. Project title and client/industry (or anonymized industry if confidential)
2. Objective
3. Challenge
4. Your personal contribution
5. Strategy and rationale
6. Results with metric, baseline, timeframe, and source
7. Evidence: approved screenshots, creative samples, links, or before/after comparisons

Suggested project types: Meta Ads campaign, social media content system, seasonal campaign concept, website/landing page. Label concept work clearly.
